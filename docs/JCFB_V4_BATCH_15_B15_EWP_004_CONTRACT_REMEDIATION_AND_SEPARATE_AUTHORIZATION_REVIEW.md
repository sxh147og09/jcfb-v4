## Preserved model selection and promotion boundaries

- Primary selection metric remains mean out-of-time Log Loss.
- Tie-break remains Brier, Log Loss variance, complexity, then lexical family ID.
- Holdout is one-time final evaluation only; it is forbidden for fitting,
  selection, and early stopping.
- Lifecycle remains `CANDIDATE -> VALIDATED -> APPROVED_FOR_ENGINE`, or
  `VALIDATED -> REJECTED`.
- Human promotion is required; automatic promotion is false.
- EWP-004 does not promote a candidate, and EWP-005 completion cannot create a
  Production release.
